The method · two axes

Magnitude tells you how much. Only the sign tells you which way.

↓ direction < 0 — machinery

Knockdown pushes cells away from the effector program. A large effect in the wrong direction — required, not druggable.

↑ direction > 0 — a brake

Knockdown pushes cells toward the effector program. Removing the gene enhances function — the therapeutic quadrant.

Axis 1 — power-equalized energy distance (causal magnitude). Axis 2 — a per-cell effector minus dysfunction score, over all 2.64 M cells.

The therapeutic quadrant

The brake quadrant recovers real drug-target biology.

CD5, DGKA — canonical inhibitory brakes, donor-consistent; DGKα and CBL-B inhibitors are already in the clinic.
SMAD3 (TGF-β), LAT2, CBLB — higher-magnitude candidates, donor-split at n=2 — a prioritized shortlist for the full four-donor cohort.
We report donor consistency honestly — the method is validated; the brake list is a lead, not an overclaim.
